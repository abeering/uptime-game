# Connections Lifecycle V1 — establishment, cancellation, and Health

**Scope:** Connections implementation in the four-node scenario. Packet is independent. This supersedes the arrival rejection and direct outage Health rules described in the earlier Blackout and Spawner extension.

A queued plan materializes as a **Connecting attempt** with an ID, route, purpose, internal classification, inspection history, and path lane. It traverses one physical route link every 0.6 simulation seconds by default. The host records intermediate and destination arrivals. Availability is checked at ingress and each reached node. A Blackout ahead does not reject a physically valid plan or cause rerouting; removal before arrival permits establishment. An offline origin produces a spawned attempt and immediate cancellation receipt. Spawner children use this same path even when their origin or route is offline. Only a physically impossible route or exhausted IDs prevents a plan from being constructed.

Connecting requests no LOAD, advances no WORK or operation behavior, and has no service deadline. Reaching the destination changes it to **Active**, starts its behaviors and deadline, and permits allocation on the following simulation segment. Large steps split at arrivals so pre-establishment time cannot become WORK. The path reveal reads the host's traveled-node and current-link progress; animation callbacks never decide establishment. Applying Blackout immediately cancels established routes through its node and Connecting routes that already reached it. Not-yet-arrived attempts continue to the node. The node still displays `OFFLINE`.

Final outcomes are **Completed**, **Killed**, and **Canceled**. The receipt retains the cause separately, currently `Player` or `Blackout`, along with phase at termination and failure node. Killed and Canceled paths, ID pills, and a present terminal marker flash bright red twice before removal. A Connecting failure flashes only the traveled part of its path. Completed connections retain their departure fade. Both timings are authored on the path prefab.

Network Health measures legitimate connection service. Blackout itself has no direct penalty or drain. A benign Killed attempt costs 10 once; a benign Canceled attempt costs 5 once, whether Connecting or Active. Hostile attempts cost zero for either outcome. Slowdown, overdue damage, and recovery use established legitimate service as before; recovery can run during an idle Blackout. The cancellation amount is provisional scene tuning.

The internal `connection_resolved` event is the complete attempt receipt: concrete definition including classification, purpose and route; attempt, optional establishment, and resolution times; outcome, cause, termination phase, failure node, and signed Health penalty. `connection_node_arrived` and `connection_established` preserve route timing. `plan_queued` still records the complete generated plan and decisions before materialization. These fields are recorder-only; ordinary inspect shows connection lifecycle status without threat intent.
